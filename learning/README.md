## Foundations to the result

The first 14 milestones connect unknown states, references, complete operations, accuracy, entropy, norms, spectral rearrangements, and dynamical resources. M14 joins them into the precise same-hardware routing comparison.

The next 16 milestones build the information-flow converse and coherent contact construction, then explain imperfect preparation, reference-preserving error, the exact/full-rank boundary, the correlated-helper counterexample, and normalization of the contact budget. M30 integrates their physical meaning and relation to prior work.

The worked examples follow the same dependencies: a Bell-reference swap whose single-qubit marginals do not change, a three-qubit encoding that restores occupied spectators, and a finite spectral concentration whose error is a joint subspace promise. Each example links to the corresponding canonical proof and retains the resource assumptions.

The map describes common understanding targets, not every specialist calculation. Primary research papers remain necessary for attribution and imported results; they do not add extra educational anchors. The [interpretation](../science/interpretation.md) brings the mechanisms together after the proof route.
